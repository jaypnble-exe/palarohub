Style guide

Design tokens and components for every screen. Phone-first, big tap targets (min 44px, primary 52px), plain words, and status is always written in text, never shown by color alone.

Inputs

Prefer tap-to-choose chips over dropdowns. Labels always above fields.

Wording

  • "Teams waiting for approval", not "Pending registrations"
  • "Saved on this device" / "Synced", not "Offline queue"
  • Every string has English and Tagalog (EN / TL toggle in header)
  • Numbers with currency: "SAR 1,800"

Commissioner

The commissioner mostly reviews and approves. Home is a to-do list, setup is a step-by-step wizard, and money is always clear. Try the EN / TL toggle on the home screen.

  • Score always visible at top; no menus during the game.
